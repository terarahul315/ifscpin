Online shopping, courier delivery, bank KYC, ya speed post bhejne ke liye sahi 6-digit PIN code hona zaroori hai. IFSCPin par aap kisi bhi gaon, shehar, mohalle ya Post Office ka Pincode, Division, District aur Delivery Status turant check kar sakte hain.

Pincode Search Karne ke 2 Tarike:

  1. 6-Digit Pincode se: Agar aapko Pincode pata hai aur uske andar aane wale sabhi Post Offices, Division aur District dekhne hain, toh 📮 Pincode tab par jaakar 6 number daalein.
  2. City / Area ke Naam se: Agar aapko Pincode nahi pata, toh 🔍 City / Area tab par apna area, block ya shehar ka naam likhein (jaise Gomti Nagar ya Jaipur) aur turant sabhi matching pincodes dekhein.

Pincode ke 6 Digits ka Matlab Kya Hota Hai?

  • Pehla digit: Bharat ka Postal Zone batata hai (1–8 regions, 9 Army Postal Service).
  • Dusra aur teesra digit: Sub-region aur sorting district dikhata hai.
  • Aakhri 3 digits: Exact Delivery Post Office ko identify karte hain.
