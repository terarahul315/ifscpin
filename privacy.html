Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Welcome to IFSCPin. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how IFSCPin handles information when you use our IFSC code and India Post Pincode lookup tool.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

IFSCPin does not require account registration, login, or personal banking credentials. We do not store your IFSC or Pincode searches on any server.

2. Local Storage

Your recent searches and language preference (English / Hindi) are saved only locally on your own device using your browser's localStorage. You can clear this anytime from your browser settings.

3. Third-Party APIs & Services

  • Bank branch and IFSC data are retrieved in real time from public APIs (such as Razorpay IFSC API).
  • Postal PIN code and area details are retrieved from India Post public lookup APIs.
  • Map links open Google Maps externally when you click them.

4. Advertising & Cookies

We may display third-party advertisements (such as Adsterra) to keep this tool free. Third-party ad partners may use cookies or standard browser signals to serve non-intrusive ads.
